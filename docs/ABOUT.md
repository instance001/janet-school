# About Janet School

## Project Identity

Janet School is a standalone research rig for studying deterministic MCM behavior inside a controlled curriculum scaffold.

It is oriented toward:

- open-source AI tooling
- cognitive scaffolding experiments
- local-first research systems

It is not presented as a consumer education product or a general chatbot application.

## Publisher And Steward

Janet School is published under Fractal Media Infrastructure, a small independent research-and-development umbrella for open-source AI tooling, cognitive scaffolding experiments, and local-first research systems.
